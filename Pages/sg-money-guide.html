The Foundation

Five moves that matter.

01

Know your cash flow

Map take-home income, fixed commitments and flexible spending before setting targets.

02

Build a buffer

Hold at least 3–6 months of expenses. Variable income may require more.

03

Protect the downside

Cover large medical bills and income shocks before optimising investments.

04

Control expensive debt

Pay credit cards in full. Rewards never compensate for revolving interest.

05

Invest consistently

Match risk to your timeline, diversify and avoid using money you will need soon.

CPF & Housing

Your home should not consume your future.

30%

HDB and EC monthly repayments are generally subject to the Mortgage Servicing Ratio.

55%

Bank lending also considers the Total Debt Servicing Ratio across monthly debt obligations.

$20k OA

CPF recommends considering an OA housing buffer instead of exhausting it automatically.

Protection

Protect income, family and recovery.

Hospitalisation

Start with protection against large treatment and hospital bills.

Death & TPD

Use 10× annual income as a broad starting benchmark—not a personal prescription.

Critical illness

5× annual income is a broad starting benchmark for recovery and an income gap.

Affordability

Aim to keep protection within 10% of take-home income.

Tax & Investing

Cash flow first. Products second.

Tax

If required to file, the YA 2026 deadline is 18 April 2026. Tax is generally due within one month of the bill.

Timeline

Keep near-term money stable. Take investment risk only with money that has time to recover.

Diversification

Spread exposure. Risk and return are linked, and concentration can magnify both.

Consistency

Start at a sustainable amount and contribute regularly instead of chasing perfect timing.
