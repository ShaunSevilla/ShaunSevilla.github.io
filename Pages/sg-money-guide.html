Timeline

Keep near-term money stable. Take investment risk only with money that has time to recover.

Diversification

Spread exposure. Risk and return are linked, and concentration can magnify both.

Consistency

Start at a sustainable amount and contribute regularly instead of chasing perfect timing.

SRS: How Much

Cap is $15,300/year (citizens/PRs). Relief starts to matter above $80k chargeable income, and becomes a clear win above $120k (the 15% bracket).

SRS: Where It Goes

Idle SRS cash earns next to nothing. Most people invest it in T-bills, Singapore Savings Bonds, unit trusts or ETFs, matched to years left until their SRS retirement age.

Credit Cards

A tool, not extra salary.

Full card picks in the bot

Pay in full, always

Card interest runs at almost 28% a year. No cashback or miles makes up for carrying a balance.

Simple cashback

UOB Absolute Cashback: 1.7% on everything, no minimum spend, no cap. Good if you don't want to track anything.

Simple miles

DBS Altitude (1.3 mpd local, 2.2 overseas, points don't expire) or UOB PRVI Miles (1.4 local, 2.4 overseas, higher fee).

Optimised miles

Up to 4 mpd, capped around $600–$1,000 a month: HSBC Revolution, UOB Preferred Visa, DBS Woman's World, Citi Rewards. Worth it only if you track categories and caps.

Read the fine print

Minimum spend (often $800/month), monthly caps, and exclusions like insurance, bills, top-ups and government payments decide what you actually earn.
