HDB Loan Calculator

HDB loan vs bank loan: downpayment, CPF usable and monthly instalment.

Shows what you pay at each stage, and how much must be cash vs CPF OA, for an HDB loan (2.6%) and a bank loan on the same flat. Doesn't check your income limits or age-based tenure.

Counts the CPF OA you'll build up in the ~3 years before key collection, so more of the downpayment comes from CPF instead of cash.

Paid into your CPF OA at key collection (BTO) or completion (resale). Leave blank if none.

CPF Growth Calculator

Projects your OA, SA/RA and MediSave balances forward over time.

Uses today's CPF contribution, allocation and interest rules, on your monthly wage only (bonus and AWS not included). At 55, your SA moves into your Retirement Account (SA first, then OA) up to the Full Retirement Sum, and MediSave is capped at the Basic Healthcare Sum. Both sums are projected to the year you turn 55.

Leave SA/MA blank (treated as $0) if you just want to project OA. CPF doesn't let you top up OA on its own, only SA/RA (via RSTU) or MediSave individually, or all three together.

Vehicle Loan Calculator

Monthly instalment, MAS loan caps and the real interest rate on a car, motorbike or private-hire loan.

Cars: MAS caps the loan at 70% of the price if the OMV is $20,000 or less, 60% if above, over at most 7 years. Motorbikes and commercial or private-hire vehicles aren't capped by MAS. Vehicle loans are quoted as a flat rate; the result also shows the real (effective) rate.

Insurance Calculator

How much cover the benchmarks say you need, what you already have, and your gap.

A simple benchmark based on your income, using the life insurance industry's guideline (9x annual income for death and total permanent disability, 4x for critical illness).
