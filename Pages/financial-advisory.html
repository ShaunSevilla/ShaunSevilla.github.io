Tax Relief Calculator

Every relief you can claim, your tax, and how much you can still save before 31 Dec.

For tax residents on a salary, using the rules for income earned this year. Assumes you claim the full child relief (you can split it with your spouse). Disability reliefs and special cases aren't included.

Already done this year? Leave blank if not.

What Can I Afford?

The home and/or car you can comfortably afford, using the rules banks actually use.

Keeps an emergency buffer untouched first: 6 months of spending, or 6 months of pay if people depend on you. "Comfortable" keeps the home loan instalment within 25% of your pay, and within the bank limits. Shows a BTO by default; tick a resale grant to see resale instead.

Grants Pick BTO or resale, not both.

HDB Loan Calculator

HDB loan vs bank loan: downpayment, CPF usable and monthly instalment.

Works out the HDB concessionary loan (2.6%, 25% downpayment) and compares it with a bank loan on the same flat (at least 5% of the price in cash). Doesn't check your Mortgage Servicing Ratio, Total Debt Servicing Ratio or age-based tenure limits.

Credited to your CPF OA at completion, so treated the same as CPF here. Leave blank or 0 if none.

CPF Growth Calculator

Projects your OA, SA/RA and MediSave balances forward over time.

Uses today's CPF contribution, allocation and interest rules, on your monthly wage only (bonus and AWS not included). At 55, your SA moves into your Retirement Account (SA first, then OA) up to the Full Retirement Sum, and MediSave is capped at the Basic Healthcare Sum. Both sums are projected to the year you turn 55.

Leave SA/MA blank (treated as $0) if you just want to project OA. CPF doesn't let you top up OA on its own, only SA/RA (via RSTU) or MediSave individually, or all three together.

Vehicle Loan Calculator

MAS loan-to-value caps and monthly instalment on a flat-rate car loan.

Uses MAS's loan-to-value caps (70% of price if OMV ≤ $20,000, else 60%) and 7-year tenure cap. Car loans are quoted as a flat rate, not reducing balance.

Insurance Calculator

How much cover you should have, and a sensible budget for it.

A simple benchmark based on your income, using the life insurance industry's guideline (9x annual income for death and total permanent disability, 4x for critical illness).

Compound Interest Calculator

See how a starting amount and regular yearly contributions could grow over time.

Estimates based on today's rules, which can change. Not personalised advice.
