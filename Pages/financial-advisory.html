Uses today's CPF contribution, allocation and interest rules.

Leave SA/MA blank (treated as $0) if you just want to project OA. Note: CPF doesn't let you voluntarily top up OA alone — only SA/RA (via RSTU) or MediSave individually, or all three together.

Vehicle Loan Calculator

MAS loan-to-value caps and monthly instalment on a flat-rate car loan.

Uses MAS's loan-to-value caps (70% of price if OMV ≤ $20,000, else 60%) and 7-year tenure cap. Car loans are quoted as a flat rate, not reducing balance.

Compound Interest Calculator

See how a starting amount and regular yearly contributions could grow over time.

Projections only, based on today's published rates and rules, which can change. Doesn't check Mortgage Servicing Ratio, Total Debt Servicing Ratio, age-based loan tenure caps, or a bank loan's lower rate but 5% minimum-cash rule. The CPF calculator uses only your stated monthly wage (excludes bonus/AWS); at 55, your Special Account moves into your Retirement Account (SA first, then OA) up to the Full Retirement Sum, and MediSave is capped at the Basic Healthcare Sum, both projected forward to the year you turn 55 rather than held at today's figures. Not personalised advice.
